# FPE Logo Assets

These three SVGs share the exact same repaired angular FPE monogram geometry. They use a compound fill path, without strokes or fragmented paths.

| File | Usage | Backdrop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fpe-mark.svg` | Signature pink (#F23D9F) | Ink / white, with sufficient contrast |
| `fpe-mark-white.svg` | White monochrome | Black (#000000) or very dark surfaces |
| `fpe-mark-black.svg` | Black monochrome | White (#FFFFFF) or very light surfaces |

Backgrounds remain **transparent** in the SVGs for use across applications. Place them on the corresponding background, rather than encoding the background inside the symbol.

Do not stretch, recolor inconsistently, crop, outline, or reconstruct the symbol.
